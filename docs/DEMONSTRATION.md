# Judge Demonstration Route

## Recommended 4–6 minute route

### 1. Introduce the challenge response (20 seconds)

Open KSA OUTPOST 86 and explain that it is a browser-based junior astronaut training mission created by Kuntau Science Academy. State that the current playable release focuses on lunar outpost systems and that its numeric meters are educational simulation values.

### 2. Enter the lunar outpost (30 seconds)

Choose **Start Mission**, review the briefing and select **Deploy to Outpost**. Show the third-person astronaut, habitat, solar array, greenhouse, shelter, rover prop and surface environment.

### 3. Demonstrate systems thinking (60–90 seconds)

Walk to the power console and inspect it. Then inspect life support. Point out that oxygen supply, cabin-air condition and carbon-dioxide control are represented separately. Complete the Day 1 objective.

### 4. Show a resource trade-off (45–60 seconds)

Advance to Day 2 and select a power plan. Explain that conservation reduces simulated demand but slows optional research/greenhouse work, while higher-demand plans consume more of the battery reserve when sunlight is limited.

### 5. Demonstrate the Mission Control assistant (45 seconds)

Open **MISSION AI**. Ask “What is my current objective?” or “Explain life support”. Then walk near a station and ask “What should I do here?” to demonstrate location-aware guidance. Mention that the assistant works offline and uses deterministic project knowledge; it is not a cloud language model.

### 6. Demonstrate science and sound (30–45 seconds)

Open **Field Systems** to show the crew illustration and surface-layer scanner. Open the Audio tab, enable a sound after the user gesture and use the radio test. Explain that the scanner is labelled as a training visual, not NASA observation data.

### 7. Close with the mission progression (30 seconds)

Explain the remaining progression: Day 3 water recovery, Day 4 shelter procedure, Day 5 greenhouse, Day 6 survey and Day 7 final assessment. Show the debrief if the mission has been completed in advance.

## Demonstration preparation

- Use a laptop with current Chrome or Edge and hardware acceleration enabled.
- Run through the mission once before the event and keep a saved checkpoint if you intend to use Continue Saved.
- Test the audio once before presenting; browser audio must be unlocked by a user action.
- Keep a second browser tab with the README and science references available for judges.
- For Android, test touch joystick, drag-to-look and the narrow-screen HUD on the actual device.

## Scope statement for judges

This archive does not yet implement Earth launch operations, spacecraft flight/landing, a Mars environment, a drivable rover or a multi-person animated 3D crew. Do not present those as working features. A future expansion can add them after the lunar mission and current controls have been validated on target devices.
