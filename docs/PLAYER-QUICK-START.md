# Quick start — KSA OUTPOST 86

1. Open the app and choose **Skip tutorial** or complete its three steps.
2. On Home, choose **5-minute demo** for the guided Mars route, or **Build my rocket** to customise a vehicle.
3. In the Rocket Workshop choose **Build my rocket automatically**, then **Launch expedition**.
4. After the launch sequence, use **W/A/S/D** to walk, **Shift** to run, **E** to interact, **F** to scan while exploring, **Space** for the movement action, and **Esc** to pause.
5. On touch devices use the on-screen joystick and action buttons. Drag the scene to look around and use pinch/scroll to zoom.
6. Approach a glowing station until the interaction hint appears. Complete oxygen, power and water tasks before advancing the mission day.
7. Walk to the rover, enter it, drive to the marked sample location, then scan when close enough.

Guest play saves on the current device. Cloud account sync is available only when the Cloudflare Worker is deployed and configured.
